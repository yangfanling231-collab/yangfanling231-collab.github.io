Welcome to Quick Brain Challenge!

This website features two simple and interactive mini games designed to challenge your brain in different ways.

🧠Memory Game

In this game, players need to remember a sequence of words shown on the screen. After the words disappear, players must recall and enter the words in the correct order. This game focuses on short-term memory, concentration, and pattern recall.


🎯Reaction Game

The Reaction Game focuses on speed and attention. Players must react quickly to visual changes, testing how fast they can respond under pressure.
